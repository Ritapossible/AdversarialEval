Model Economy · paid benchmark

What is a paid security answer worth?

We bought the same security questions from every reachable Telegraph miner, under rules fixed before the first call, and reconciled every payment. The result tells a buyer which providers are worth paying, and how far the default route can be trusted.

Accuracy across three paid rounds

3 rounds, 3,680 paid calls, testnet

We bought the same 46 security questions from every reachable Telegraph miner on three days, under a rule fixed before the first call, and reconciled the payments with the chain or the node's ledger (one $0.01 charge unmatched).

How many providers are really competing?

public listing, 24 Nov
Listed miners

Payout addresses
13 distinct. Two addresses run 26 of the 37 miners (18 and 8).
In our rounds
The 32 miners that answered in round 4 belong to 9 payout identities; in the Solana run, the ranked route sent 19 of 21 questions to one miner.
Why it matters
When one operator runs many miners, a "second opinion" can come from the same source, and agreement between miners says less than it seems.

Addresses are an upper bound on independence: one team can use several. Method and data.
