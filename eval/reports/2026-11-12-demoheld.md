## Reading

- **Removing 24 pairs (mostly hand-written) cost about 6 points on validation**, mainly on
  hand-written validation pairs (81% → 70.5%). Data volume in the hand-written families
  still matters at this size, which supports getting more independent, human-written data
  (U1) over more same-author generation.
- The demo numbers rest on 17 pairs (95% CIs about ±20 points). They illustrate the idea and
  aren't evidence of accuracy.
- The page's chart shows both adapters: the full-data one (72–75%) and the held-out one
  used in the game (64–71%), labelled as such.
